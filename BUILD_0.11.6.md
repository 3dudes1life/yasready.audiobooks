# YasReady Audiobooks 0.11.6 — Residual Review Finalizer

## Goal
Close the remaining safe Book One speaker-resolution gaps while separating permanent Audio Bible continuity from one-scene extras.

## Changes
- Scene-local extras no longer receive reusable series character keys.
- Derek is treated as a New Year’s Eve scene extra, not a permanent/canonical series character.
- The unnamed woman in that scene is `New Year’s Couple – Woman`, not `Derek’s Girlfriend`.
- Adds broader post-dialogue attribution and conservative explicit lead resolution.
- Character-plan exports now show `continuity_scope` (`book` or `scene`).
- Scene-local roles remain bindable for audio production but are marked to be excluded from series continuity inheritance.
- Paid generation remains disabled.
